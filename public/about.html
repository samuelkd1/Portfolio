About Me

I’m Sam, a data and technology professional with a strong interest in boxing, fitness and self-improvement. I like being organised and using data to understand what is actually happening, whether that’s at work or in my own life. I’m naturally drawn to spotting patterns, trends and things I might otherwise overlook.

In my main job, I use tools like Tableau, SQL, Python, Power Apps and Excel to solve problems, automate work and make information easier to understand. Outside of that, I work part-time as a bouncer, which has helped me build confidence, communication skills and the ability to stay calm under pressure.

I carry the same mentality into almost everything I do. Whether it’s work, training, money, fitness or my routines, I like to track progress, understand what is working and find ways to improve.
